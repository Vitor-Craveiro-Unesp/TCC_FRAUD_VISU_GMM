8. Normality tests

The Shapiro-Wilk and Jarque-Bera tests were applied to evaluate whether the empirical distribution of SEND_AGE is statistically compatible with a normal distribution.

The complete set of valid observations was used. No random sampling was performed.

H1: The feature does not follow a normal distribution.
