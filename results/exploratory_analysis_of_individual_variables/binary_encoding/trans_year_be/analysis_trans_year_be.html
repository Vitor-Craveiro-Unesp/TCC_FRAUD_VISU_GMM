4. Imbalance Ratio (IR)

The Imbalance Ratio was calculated as the ratio between the number of observations in the most frequent year and the number of observations in the least frequent year.

A value close to 1 indicates similar frequencies between the two years. Progressively larger values indicate a greater difference between the number of observations.

Shannon Entropy provides a measure of how balanced the distribution of observations is between the two years.
